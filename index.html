Why this exists

Most scheduling tools make someone guess a list of dates first, then ask everyone to vote on them. That works for a one-hour meeting. It falls apart when you want “a long weekend sometime in the next three months”.

Typical date polls

Propose options, then vote

The organizer adds every possible date by hand. Everyone checks each one. If the perfect weekend wasn’t on the list, nobody ever finds out.

Only the dashed days were ever asked about.
When Can We Go?

Mark what you can’t do, and that’s it

Pick a broad range. Everyone taps the few days they really can’t make it (or “maybe not”). The app checks every day and finds the free stretches for you.

Every day is covered. Green is a window where nobody is blocked.
Your calendar says you’re busy every single day. We ask which days you really can’t do.

That’s why there’s deliberately no calendar import. A recurring stand-up or a dentist appointment you can move shouldn’t kill the team offsite. Only you know which blockers are real, so you mark those, and the “maybe not” option covers the ones you’d rather avoid but could shift.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no ads, and nothing to unlock.

Does anyone need an account?

No, not even the organizer. You create an event, get a link and share it. People open the link and add their name.

How is this different from Doodle or When2meet?

Those tools ask you to vote on dates someone proposed, or to paint every hour you’re free. Here you only mark the days you can’t make it across a whole range, and the app finds the free stretches. It’s built for days and multi-day windows, not one-hour slots.

Why can’t I import my calendar?

Because a busy calendar has something on almost every day. Whether that stand-up or appointment actually stops you from going is something only you can decide, so you mark the days that really matter.

Who can see my answers?

Everyone who has the event link can see and edit the event, so only share it with the people you’re planning with. A first name or nickname is enough. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I delete an event or a person?

You can remove a person and their markings directly in the event. To delete a whole event, get in touch via the imprint and include the link.
